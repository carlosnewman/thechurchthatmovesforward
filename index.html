What You'll Discover

01

The Baptism with the Holy Spirit

Acts 1:1-9

Introduces Jesus' final instructions, focusing on the promise of the outpouring of the Spirit. This divine act empowers the church with power to witness the Gospel and purifies the heart.

02

The Message of the Church

Acts 1:1-11

Analyzes Luke's account, establishing the pillars of what the church should preach. The message must be founded on what Jesus did and taught, His resurrection, and His return.

03

Starting Well and Finishing Poorly

Acts 1:16-17

Through the tragic trajectory of Judas, this chapter warns about spiritual superficiality and shows how small concessions to sin culminate in ruin.

04

The "Suddenlys" of God

Acts 2:1-2

Explores how God intervenes in sudden and transforming ways. Whether in the fulfillment of promises, salvation, or deliverances, God acts when the church positions itself in faith.

05

What Does This Mean?

Acts 2:1-13

Details the characteristics of the Holy Spirit's manifestation on the day of Pentecost. The work is celestial, full, and supernatural, aiming to empower ordinary believers.

06

The Pitiful Peter, or the Worthy Peter?

Acts 2:14-41

Contrasting the fearful Peter of the Gospels with the intrepid Peter of Acts, this study highlights the transforming power of the Spirit in human lives.
