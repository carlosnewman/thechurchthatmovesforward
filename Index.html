Frequently asked questions

Are the e-books digital?

Yes. All five e-books are delivered digitally.

Can I use this material in small groups?

Yes. All five e-books include a guide with questions for Small Groups and Sunday School.

Is the content based on Acts?

Yes. This collection is a message and study series on the book of Acts: E-book 1 covers Acts 1–2, E-book 2 continues through Acts 2–4, E-book 3 advances through Acts 5–7, E-book 4 moves into Acts 8–9, and E-book 5 concludes with Acts 10–12.

Do I need to read them in order?

It's recommended. Each e-book picks up right where the previous one leaves off — from the outpouring of the Spirit (E-book 1), through a community in motion (E-book 2), a faith forged in trial (E-book 3), a church sent out on mission (E-book 4), to the expansion of grace across every cultural barrier (E-book 5).

What's new in E-book 5?

E-book 5, "The Expansion of Grace and the Borderless Gospel," covers Cornelius's conversion, the Spirit falling on the Gentiles, the first believers called "Christians" in Antioch, and Peter's miraculous release through prayer (Acts 10–12).
